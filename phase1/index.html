⚙️ Workflow

How It Works

From patient arrival to treated and recorded — a seamless four-step process.

01
📋

Patient Registers

Receptionist fills in the patient's name, symptoms, pain severity, breathing difficulty, and duration.

02
⚡

Score Calculated

An urgency score is instantly computed. Critical symptoms like chest pain or severe bleeding are prioritised.

03
🩺

Doctor Consults

The highest-priority patient is called. Doctor reviews today's symptoms plus full past visit history.

04
✅

Visit Recorded

Doctor adds treatment notes and finishes the visit. Record is saved. Next patient is auto-called.

✨ Features

Everything Your Clinic Needs

Built to handle real hospital workflows with simplicity and intelligence.

📋

Smart Intake Form

Log symptoms, pain level (1–5), breathing difficulty (1–5), and duration in one clean, fast interface.

⚡

Auto Triage Scoring

Each patient gets an urgency score computed instantly. Emergency cases are automatically pushed to the top.

🔄

Anti-Starvation Queue

Patients waiting too long receive automatic priority boosts so no one is ever left behind in the queue.

🩺

Doctor's Full View

Doctors see today's symptoms, full past visit history, and write treatment notes — all in one place.

📊

Admin Dashboard

Live stats, patient history search by phone number, and complete staff account management.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Admin, Doctor, and Receptionist each have separate, secure dashboards with only what they need.

👥 Staff Roles

Built for Every Team Member

Three dedicated portals — each tailored to the exact needs of that role.

📞

Receptionist

Registers arriving patients, fills in symptoms and vitals, and monitors the live priority queue in real time.

🩺

Doctor

Views the current patient's full profile, checks past visit history, records diagnosis notes, and marks the visit as complete.

🔧

Administrator

Oversees clinic stats, searches patient records by phone, and manages all staff accounts from one dashboard.
